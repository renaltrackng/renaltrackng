About us

A digital health solution for earlier kidney-damage risk screening.

RenalTrack is a digital health solution focused on improving access to early kidney-damage risk screening in Nigeria. We make community-based screening more accessible through pharmacies and Community Health Workers (CHWs), helping people identify possible signs of kidney damage and seek appropriate medical assessment.

{% icon 'badge-check' %}

Who we are

RenalTrack Limited provides a digital approach to community-based kidney-health screening, combining urine albumin testing with a platform that supports patient registration, health-risk documentation and screening-result recording.

What we offer

Everything a community screening programme needs.

From the test itself to the record it leaves behind and the follow-up that comes after.

{% icon 'flask-conical' %}

Community-based kidney screening

Urine albumin screening conducted by pharmacists and trained Community Health Workers to help identify possible signs of kidney damage.

{% icon 'notebook-pen' %}

Digital screening records

A digital platform that supports authorised screening workers in registering patients, documenting relevant health information and recording screening results.

{% icon 'clipboard-check' %}

Screening information management

A structured digital workflow for recording and handling screening information to support appropriate follow-up.

{% icon 'route' %}

Referral support

Support for appropriate referral when screening results indicate that further medical assessment may be necessary.

Who we serve

Built for the people closest to the community.

RenalTrack serves the general population through community pharmacies and Community Health Workers — including people with and without known risk factors for kidney disease.

How RenalTrack works

Four clear steps, from registration to follow-up.

  1. 01{% icon 'user-plus' %}

    Patient registration

    The pharmacist or Community Health Worker registers the patient and records relevant health information.

  2. 02{% icon 'flask-conical' %}

    Urine albumin screening

    The test is conducted using the appropriate urine albumin test strip, following the manufacturer's instructions.

  3. 03{% icon 'notebook-pen' %}

    Digital record-keeping

    The screening worker records the patient's screening information and test result on the RenalTrack platform.

  4. 04{% icon 'route' %}

    Appropriate follow-up

    Screening information supports appropriate next steps and referral for further clinical assessment where necessary.

Medical information

RenalTrack supports kidney-damage risk screening, not definitive diagnosis. Screening results may indicate possible kidney damage but cannot independently confirm or rule out chronic kidney disease. Further clinical assessment and confirmatory testing may be required.
